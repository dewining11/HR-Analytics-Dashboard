# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard was developed to analyze employee attrition patterns and workforce demographics. The dashboard helps HR teams monitor employee retention, identify high-risk groups based on age, gender, department, education background, and job satisfaction, and support data-driven workforce management decisions.

## Tools Used

- Tableau
- Excel

## Key Metrics

- Employee Count: 1,470
- Active Employees: 1,233
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16%

## Key Insights

- The overall attrition rate is 16%.
- R&D department experiences the highest attrition.
- Most employees are between 30–36 years old.
- Life Sciences records the highest attrition among education fields.
- Employees aged 25–34 contribute the largest proportion of attrition cases.

## Business Value

The dashboard enables HR departments to identify workforce trends, monitor employee turnover, and develop targeted retention strategies.
